Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the CRNA Vacation Draft application, operated by Brad Lord ("the Brand"), collects, uses, and protects information.

What information we collect

We collect the name and mobile phone number of CRNA staff members who participate in the internal vacation scheduling draft. This information is entered directly by the practice administrator, not collected from the public.

How we use this information

Your name and phone number are used solely to operate the internal vacation scheduling draft: to identify your turn in the draft order, to send you an SMS text message notifying you when it is your turn to select a vacation week, and to record your selection.

Data sharing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is used exclusively for internal scheduling purposes within the practice and is not shared outside the practice except as required to deliver the SMS notifications themselves (via our messaging provider, Twilio).

Opting out

Staff members may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP, or by notifying the practice administrator directly to be removed from the roster.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the practice administrator, Brad Lord.
