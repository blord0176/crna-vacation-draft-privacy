These Terms & Conditions govern use of the CRNA Vacation Draft SMS notification program, operated by Brad Lord ("the Brand").

Program description

This program sends internal staff scheduling notifications to CRNA staff participating in an internal vacation scheduling draft. Staff members are notified by text message when it is their turn to select a vacation week, and are provided a link to make their selection.

SMS Terms

By participating, you agree to receive text messages related to the vacation scheduling draft. Message frequency varies based on draft activity and your position in the draft order; most participants will receive a small number of messages during an active draft period.

Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP at any time to opt out of receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact the practice administrator, Brad Lord, directly.

Eligibility

This program is limited to CRNA staff members added to the roster by the practice administrator. It is not open to the general public.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be directed to the practice administrator, Brad Lord.
